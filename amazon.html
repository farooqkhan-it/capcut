What is Amazon?

Amazon is one of the world's largest e-commerce and technology companies. Founded by Jeff Bezos in 1994, Amazon started as an online bookstore and gradually expanded into a global marketplace offering millions of products and services.

Today, customers can explore electronics, fashion, books, home appliances, beauty products, groceries and much more through its online shopping platform.

How Does Amazon Work?

Amazon connects customers with products through its online marketplace. Visitors can search for products, compare prices, read customer reviews and place orders through the website or mobile application.

Its shopping experience is designed to make discovering and purchasing products convenient and accessible.

Popular Amazon Services

Amazon offers several services beyond online shopping. These include Amazon Prime, Amazon Web Services (AWS), Kindle, Amazon Music and digital entertainment services.

Benefits of Online Shopping

Online shopping makes it easier to discover different products, compare available options and shop without visiting multiple physical stores.
